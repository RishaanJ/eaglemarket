# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students in one school community who want a fun, low-friction way to make predictions about classes, campus events, clubs, sports, SPW, and other shared school moments.

## Product Purpose

EagleMarket turns school questions into understandable, play-token prediction markets. EAG tokens have no monetary value and exist only for picks, streaks, status, and friendly leaderboards. Success means a student can grasp the current probability, understand how a market resolves, and make a Yes or No prediction with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Confident, approachable, and alert. EagleMarket should feel credible enough for consequential questions while staying lively and understandable for people who do not consider themselves traders.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense institutional terminals, crypto-casino spectacle, gamified confetti, jargon-heavy order tickets, and pixel-for-pixel imitation of Kalshi. Do not make casual users decode professional trading conventions.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the question and its plain-language probability.
- Explain the contract before asking for commitment.
- Make Yes and No actions obvious, calm, and reversible.
- Use market activity to build confidence, not urgency.
- Keep advanced detail available without making it the default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal compliance target is required for the initial concept. Maintain clear keyboard focus, readable contrast, semantic controls, reduced-motion support, and labels that do not rely on color alone as sensible product defaults.
